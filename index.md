# Embodied Learning Lab

The Embodied Learning Lab is a research lab in the Robotics Department at the University of Michigan.

*Robot learning and tactile perception for robust embodied agents.*

## Research Themes

- Tactile perception for contact-rich manipulation
- Sim-to-real transfer of learned policies
- Shared autonomy for human-robot collaboration
